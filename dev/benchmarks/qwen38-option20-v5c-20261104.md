# Option 20 V5c: a 9G cap buys prefill speed and spends the residency that 131k context needs

Measured 2026-11-04 on the MacBook Air (M5, 10 cores, 16 GB unified memory), model
`ISTA-DASLab/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GSQ-RCO-GGUF:IQ3_XXS` with the DFlash2 draft, served on port 1235 as
`qwen3.8-flash-next-iq3-xxs-splash`.

```sh
SPLASH_FLASH_NEXT_MAX_MEMORY=9G SPLASH_FLASH_NEXT_MAX_ACTIVE_REQUESTS=2 \
SPLASH_FLASH_NEXT_SECOND_LANE_BELOW_GATE=1 bash /Users/mymac/startllamacpp.sh --direct 20
```

Engine argv: `131072 9663676416 17179869184 --persistent-cache on --cache-dir
~/Library/Caches/Splash/flash-next-option20-iq3-xxs --prefill-chunk-tokens 1024 --state-format bf16
--resource-wait-timeout 60 --ple-memory 67108864`. Context and the 16 GiB cache quota are unchanged
from V5; the cap is one GiB lower. Rows come from
`build/validation/option20-v5c-20261104/run_v5c.py`, the V5 harness copied forward. Condensed data:
`dev/benchmarks/qwen38-option20-v5c-20261104.json`.

`28k-cold` and `28k-hit` were `clean`/`foreign_active_samples: 0`, and the server agreed with the
parent row independently: `Done · input 28,589 · prefill 28,589/156.24s · PP 183.0 tok/s ·
decode 113/9.66s · TPS 11.7 tok/s`. Across the series the cold 28,589-token prefill has now run
150.7, 154.3, 161.0, 166.4, 174.6 and 183.0 tok/s as the cap came down from 11G to 9G, while decode

much of the machine the plan reserves; decode does not, because decode is limited by the 5-6 GB/s the
drive supplies.

`host_headroom_bytes` never reached the 1 GiB the governor requires. It peaked at 0.52 GiB during the

unused, so the peak headroom went *down* against V5's 0.80 GiB:

## What 9G spends: the residency the served context depends on

The startup plan says it plainly:

```
Long requests keep their KV on the SSD cache: 262144 tokens of context
(bound: model; window 262144, resident 114681, spilled 262144, disk 262144)
```

Resident KV is 114,681 tokens, which is **below the 131,072 context this profile serves**. A single
full-length request therefore spills 16,391 tokens to the SSD tier as designed, and a second lane
(262,144 tokens) has nothing to run in. The residency ladder across the three caps:

| Cap | resident KV tokens | covers served 131,072? | covers two lanes at 131,072? | host headroom peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11G | 262,137 | yes | yes (262,144 by plan) | 0.26 GiB |
| 10G | 196,601 | yes | no | 0.80 GiB |
| 9G | 114,681 | **no** | no | 0.52 GiB |

Each GiB of cap is about 2,407 KV pages, about 77,000 tokens at 13.24 KiB per token, which is exactly
the spacing in that table. The two requirements are therefore mutually exclusive on 16 GB: the
resident KV for two 131,072-token requests needs roughly the 11 GiB cap, and the governor's 1 GiB of
host headroom needs the cap low enough that the host keeps 3.147 GiB spare. At every cap tried, the
engine had between 0.00 and 0.80 GiB of host headroom while loaded, and 2.1-2.9 GiB of swap in use.

So `--max-active-requests 2` at 131,072 context is not a knob to be tuned on this Mac. It is reached
either by more RAM (24 GB or more, which is what the launcher's gate already encodes) or by a context
small enough that both residency and headroom fit - which is not this profile.

## Where that leaves the profile

Revert to the 10G default. It keeps residency above the served context (196,601 tokens against
131,072), leaves the most host headroom of the three settings (2.26 GiB spare at idle, 0.80 GiB peak
headroom), and its cold prefill is within 10% of 9G. The 9G measurement still matters as data: it
shows prefill rising to 183 tok/s as the plan reserves less, which is the strongest evidence yet that
prefill on this Mac is limited by reservation pressure rather than by compute.

The last untried lever for the second lane is the host's own tenants. At the 10G cap the peak headroom
was 0.80 GiB against a 1 GiB margin - 0.2 GiB short - and the machine's other processes held Chrome
and its helpers 1.42 GiB, LM Studio 0.39, cmux 0.37, a Python process 0.31, Activity Monitor 0.29,
opencode 0.26. That gap can be closed without a restart and without touching the plan.
